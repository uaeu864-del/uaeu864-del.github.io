Privacy Policy — W360 Uploader

Last updated: 17 November 2026

W360 Uploader is a private, internal tool used only by the W360 News channel to manage its own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Use of YouTube API Services

W360 Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

What information it accesses, collects and stores

With the account owner's OAuth permission, it uses the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs and the Google Drive API to: upload the channel's own videos; set their titles, descriptions, tags, localizations and thumbnails; add them to the channel's own playlists; post and read comments on the channel's own videos; and read the channel's own video and channel statistics. It uploads the channel's own video files to the channel's own Google Drive folder.

It stores only an OAuth refresh token (kept privately in the operator's secure environment, never published) and the channel's own video metadata and statistics needed for its work. It does not collect data about other users or viewers, and does not use cookies or collect device information.

How information is used and shared

Information is used only to perform the actions above for the W360 News channel. It is not sold, not shared with any third party, and not used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Statistics stored by the tool are refreshed regularly and deleted or refreshed within 30 days. Stored data can be deleted at any time on request via the contact below.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

W360 Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

W360 News — via the YouTube channel https://www.youtube.com/@W360News or by email at w360news@gmail.com.
