W360 News

W360 News publishes Arabic news analysis videos on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

W360 Uploader

W360 Uploader is the channel's private, internal tool. It uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API) to upload the channel's own videos to its own YouTube channel, set their titles, descriptions, tags, translations and thumbnails, add them to the channel's playlists, post the channel's own comment under its videos, and read the channel's own statistics. It also uploads the channel's video files to its own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
